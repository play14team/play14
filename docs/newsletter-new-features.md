# What's new on play14? Your community just got a massive upgrade! 🎉

Hey there, playful people!

Remember when our website looked like it was still living in 2015? Well, we've been busy behind the scenes, and we're excited to share all the shiny new things we've built for you. Whether you're a player looking for your next event or an organizer planning the next big gathering, we've got treats for everyone!

## For players: your new playground 🎮

### Find what you're looking for (finally!)

Remember spending ages scrolling to find that one event or player? Those days are over:

- **Lightning-fast search**: Hit Command+K (or Ctrl+K on Windows) and boom—search anything, anywhere. Events, players, games, articles... it's all there. We call it the command palette, but you can call it magic.
- **Smart filters**: Looking for events in a specific year? Want to see only facilitators in your area? Our new filtering system lets you slice and dice the community however you like, and your filters stick in the URL so you can bookmark and share your favorite views.
- **Alphabetical navigation**: Browse players by name with handy letter jump buttons. No more endless scrolling through hundreds of profiles.

### Pages that actually look good

We gave our pages a complete makeover. Everything's cleaner, easier to read, and actually works on your phone:

- **Event pages**: Big, beautiful layouts that put all the important info right where you need it—dates, location, registration, the whole deal.
- **Player profiles**: Your profile now looks like it belongs in 2026, not 2006. Clean layout, easy-to-read info, and it shows off your #play14 journey beautifully.
- **Game pages**: Every game in our library got a fresh coat of paint with better descriptions and easier navigation.
- **Article pages**: Our blog posts and articles are now a joy to read with better typography and layout.

### Things that just work™

- **Mobile-friendly everything**: Seriously, we tested this on phones, tablets, and everything in between. It all works now.
- **Navigation you can actually use**: Our menu makes sense now. Plus there's a handy dropdown on the homepage to jump to any section.
- **Progress indicator**: That subtle line at the top shows you when pages are loading. Small touch, big difference.
- **Scroll to top button**: When you're deep in a page, just click the button to zoom back to the top. Your thumb will thank you.

### Your profile, your control

Big news: you can now take charge of your own profile!

- **Create your account**: Sign up directly on the platform and claim your profile.
- **Edit your info**: Update your bio, location, skills, and preferences anytime you want. No more waiting for admins to make changes for you.
- **Buy tickets directly**: See an event you love? Purchase tickets right on the website without leaving the platform.
- **Track your journey**: Your profile automatically keeps track of the events you've attended and your role in the community.

**Coming soon**: We're rolling out invitations progressively to all existing players. You'll receive your personal invite to join the platform very soon! But if you're eager to get started (or need to grab tickets right away), you can sign up now and claim your profile—no need to wait.

### The "likes" wall of love

We added a special page showcasing all the lovely things people have said about #play14 on LinkedIn. It's heartwarming stuff, and it's all real testimonials from your fellow players.

### Home sweet home

Our homepage got a complete reorganization with:
- **Interactive map**: See where #play14 events are happening around the world. Click on locations to explore past and upcoming events in each city. It's like a visual history of our global community.
- **Stats that matter**: See how many events we've hosted, how many players we've brought together, and how many hosts make it all happen.
- **FAQ section**: Common questions, answered before you even ask them.
- **Better layout**: Everything flows better now. We promise you'll find what you need faster.

## For organizers: your admin superpowers 💪

### Native ticketing that doesn't suck

This is the big one. You can now sell tickets directly through the platform:

- **Stripe Connect integration**: Connect your own Stripe account and receive payments directly to your bank account. No middleman, no waiting, no nonsense.
- **Multiple ticket types**: Early bird, standard, VIP—set up as many ticket types as you want with different prices and availability windows.
- **Real-time availability**: Players see exactly how many tickets are left. When they're gone, they're gone.
- **Secure payments**: Bank transfers, cards, local payment methods—it all works, and Stripe handles the security.
- **Automatic invoices**: Every purchase generates a proper invoice automatically. Tax compliance? We got you.
- **Discount codes**: Want to reward your early supporters or offer special pricing? Create discount codes with a few clicks.

### Event management that makes sense

Running an event just got way easier:

- **Tabbed editor**: Creating or editing an event is now organized into clear sections—no more endless scrolling.
- **Media management**: Upload images, manage your event gallery, all in one place.
- **Publish control**: Events stay in draft mode until you're ready to share them with the world.
- **Budget tracking**: Keep tabs on your event finances right in the platform.
- **Attendee management**: See who's coming, track registrations, manage your player list.

### Email automation that works for you

We built a complete email system so you can focus on running events, not chasing people:

- **Organizer notifications**: Get instant alerts when someone registers, when tickets sell out, or when something needs your attention.
- **Player invitations**: Send invites to potential players directly from the admin panel. Track who you've invited and their status.
- **Results reminders**: Automated reminders to share your event results and photos afterward. No more forgetting to close the loop.
- **Beautiful templates**: All emails look professional and on-brand. No more ugly plain text messages.

### The admin dashboard of your dreams

Your admin experience got a complete overhaul:

- **Player management**: Filter by role (facilitator, player, coach), see invitation status, bulk invite people to join the platform.
- **Venue management**: Add and manage locations with all the details you need.
- **Rich text editor**: Write event descriptions with headings, blockquotes, horizontal rules, and proper formatting. The editor is now actually pleasant to use.
- **Optimistic updates**: When you change something, the UI updates instantly. No more waiting for spinners.
- **Dark mode support**: Late-night event planning? We've got your eyes covered.

### Behind-the-scenes magic

You won't see these, but they make everything better:

- **Automated cron jobs**: Regular maintenance tasks run automatically with distributed locking (fancy way of saying "they don't step on each other's toes").
- **Error reporting**: When something goes wrong, we know about it immediately and can fix it fast.
- **Performance monitoring**: We track how fast everything loads and constantly optimize.
- **Health checks**: The system monitors itself and alerts us if anything's off.
