# Open

<p class="word-tagline">The event belongs to the participants.</p>

#play14 is open in many ways. Anyone can take part, and anyone can propose a session.

Nobody prepares the agenda months in advance. The program comes from the people who are there, with the ideas, games, questions and experiments they want to explore. That gives the event a different atmosphere, driven by curiosity instead of a fixed schedule, and the event follows the energy and interests of the people in the room.

I think of the [first event](../beginnings/the-first-event.md). On the last day many people had flights home, and only a small group was left. We dropped the marketplace and spent the whole afternoon doing improv theater. It was one of the funniest afternoons I've ever spent. Nobody had planned it, and that's exactly why it was so good.
