# In 14 words

> We are a worldwide gathering of like-minded people who believe that playing is the best way to learn, share and foster creativity!

That's the sentence we use to describe #play14. It's accurate, but it doesn't tell you what it's like to be there.

In March 2026 I was invited to speak at GamiCon 38. I recorded the talk on March 14th, exactly twelve years after the first event in Luxembourg. I called it *#play14 in 14 words*: fourteen words, each with one sentence to explain it.

| Word | In one sentence |
| --- | --- |
| [Playful](playful.md) | Play lowers barriers and makes people open to learning |
| [Learning](learning.md) | The game is merely an excuse for a debrief |
| [Metaphor](metaphor.md) | The game becomes a mirror of reality |
| [Open](open.md) | The event belongs to the participants |
| [Format](format.md) | The best sessions are the ones people care about |
| [Flat](flat.md) | Everyone is both learner and teacher |
| [Food](food.md) | The conference continues at the table |
| [Affordable](affordable.md) | Learning through play should be accessible |
| [Non-profit](non-profit.md) | The goal is not revenue, it's community |
| [Brand](brand.md) | The logo connects events around the world |
| [People](people.md) | It's not about games, it's about people |
| [Diversity](diversity.md) | We celebrate the diversity of our people by being inclusive and respectful of all origins, cultures, languages, or beliefs |
| [Organic](organic.md) | We grow slowly and organically, by meeting passionate people who want to bring game-based learning to their hometown |
| [Global](global.md) | We started small but spread across the world |

The chapters that follow take each word in turn.
