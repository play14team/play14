# Diversity

<p class="word-tagline">We celebrate the diversity of our people by being inclusive and respectful of all origins, cultures, languages, or beliefs.</p>

Diversity has been celebrated at #play14 from the start.

People come with different backgrounds, professions, cultures and levels of experience, and everyone is welcome. You don't need to be an expert in games or facilitation to take part. You don't even need to bring a game: it's perfectly fine to come to #play14 and just play.

The environment is welcoming and built on psychological safety, so people feel comfortable trying things and sharing ideas. Everyone takes part at their own pace.

This diversity of perspectives makes the experience richer for everyone. It's also what we saw in [Beirut](../growth/cities/beirut.md) back in 2015: learning through play works across cultures.
