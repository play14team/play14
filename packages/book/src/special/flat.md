# Flat

<p class="word-tagline">Everyone is both learner and teacher.</p>

#play14 is flat by design. There are no speakers on one side and an audience on the other. Everyone takes part, and everyone can contribute.

Someone who attends one session may facilitate the next. There's no hierarchy, no experts on stage, no rock stars.

Learning happens peer to peer, through shared experiences and conversations. Knowledge flows in every direction, and everyone is both a learner and a teacher.

You can see it in the marketplace too. It's fine, even encouraged, to propose a game someone else has run before, and over the years some have become #play14 classics. We've seen people discover a game as players one day and facilitate it the next.
