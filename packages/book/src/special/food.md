# Food

<p class="word-tagline">The conference continues at the table.</p>

I'm French, so food matters a lot in my culture, and by design it matters at #play14 too.

All the players share their meals, which leaves room for informal conversations. Those moments help people connect and reflect on what they've just experienced together.

Lunch and dinner are a chance to get to know each other outside the sessions, and that's often where the community bonds form.

Some of the best ideas, collaborations and future games start at the table.
