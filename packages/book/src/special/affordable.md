# Affordable

<p class="word-tagline">Learning through play should be accessible.</p>

#play14 is designed to be affordable. We keep ticket prices low so that as many people as possible can come. Accessibility matters to this community.

Volunteers organize every event and give their time and energy to make it happen. The aim is to remove barriers so people can come, play, learn and share without cost getting in the way.

The ticket covers the venue, the meals, the game materials and the goodies. You pay for what you get, and nothing more.
