# Brand

<p class="word-tagline">The logo connects events around the world.</p>

#play14 is also a brand: simple, and easy to recognize. The [logo Christian Jolas designed](../beginnings/a-name-and-a-logo.md) in 2014 reflects where the community comes from, along with its openness and playfulness.

Over time it has become a symbol linking events in many countries. Some teams have adapted it, especially in Asia, but the core of the logo stays the same.

Each event is organized locally, yet they all share the same spirit and identity. The brand stands for playful learning, collaboration, and a global community whose members feel like one family.
