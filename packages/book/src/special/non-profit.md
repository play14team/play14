# Non-profit

<p class="word-tagline">The goal is not revenue, it's community.</p>

#play14 is a non-profit initiative. The community drives it, not a commercial organization.

Any profit from an event goes back into future #play14 events and the community around them. The hosting guide says it is either reinvested in future events, returned to the global organization to support other teams, or spent on games and materials.

There's no commercial agenda and no product to sell. The focus is on learning, sharing ideas, and helping people explore playful approaches to work and collaboration.
