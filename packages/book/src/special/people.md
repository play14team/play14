# People

<p class="word-tagline">It's not about games, it's about people.</p>

#play14 is really about the people.

Participants come from all kinds of professions: facilitators, coaches, trainers, teachers, developers and many other curious professionals. We've even had street performers, psychologists and dental hygienists.

What they share is a willingness to experiment, to try new things, and to learn together through play.

No central organizer creates the event. It comes from the people who attend. The community brings the ideas, the games and the energy that make each event unique.

Personally, #play14 has let me travel to amazing places and meet wonderful people all over the world, many of whom I now count as good friends. It really does feel like a family.

As one of the experiences on our website puts it: *you can discover more about a person in an hour of play than in a year of conversation.*
