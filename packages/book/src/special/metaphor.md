# Metaphor

<p class="word-tagline">The game becomes a mirror of reality.</p>

Games often work as metaphors. A simple activity can stand for a much more complicated real-world situation, and through it people experience things like teamwork, leadership or systems thinking.

Because it's a metaphor, the stakes are low. It's a safe space to experiment, try things out, and even fail.

And complex ideas become tangible. People stop discussing abstract concepts. They see them and feel them, then reflect on them together.
