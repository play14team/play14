# Global

<p class="word-tagline">We started small but spread across the world.</p>

The last word is *global*. Today #play14 is a global community.

Events take place in many countries and bring together people from all over the world who share an interest in playful learning.

Local volunteers organize each event, so every edition has its own flavor. But for all those local differences, they share the same spirit and values.

Over time this has built a growing worldwide network of people connected through play and learning. It began with three people in a hotel bar in Luxembourg who couldn't get tickets to someone else's conference.
