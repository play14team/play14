# Organic

<p class="word-tagline">We grow slowly and organically, by meeting passionate people who want to bring game-based learning to their hometown.</p>

Since 2014, #play14 has grown organically.

We don't try to make events bigger. The community grows wider instead, with new events appearing in different places around the world.

It usually starts when someone who loves game-based learning discovers #play14 and wants to bring it to their hometown. We never push anyone to organize an event. They come to us when they're interested, and the community supports them as they become #play14 hosts. That support is the [mentoring program](../mentoring/why-mentoring.md).

Players usually find #play14 through word of mouth and their networks. That keeps the growth natural and driven by the community.
