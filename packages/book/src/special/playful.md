# Playful

<p class="word-tagline">Play lowers barriers and makes people open to learning.</p>

The first word was always going to be *playful*.

At #play14, play is how we learn. People don't sit through lectures. They learn by doing. Fun isn't a side effect. It's the method. We use physical games, activities and small experiments to explore ideas together.

Play also fills a room with energy. People get involved quickly, and the barriers between them come down. Once people have played together, conversation gets easier, collaboration comes naturally, and they learn almost without noticing.
