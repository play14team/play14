# Asia and beyond, 2023–2026

In 2023 nine events took place, with new teams in Zagreb, Curia in Portugal and Zürich. The biggest news came from Asia. In May, #play14 Manila became the first event in Asia since the pandemic, at the Asian Institute of Management Conference Centre. In November, Kuala Lumpur came back.

In 2024, India joined the map with Bangalore, and Singapore held its first edition. With Figino Serenza, Florence and Zoetermeer in Europe, it was our biggest year: 14 events, matching 2019.

From 2025 on, much of the growth came from Asia. Pune and Bangkok joined in 2025 (Bangkok had been canceled in 2020, and it finally happened), along with Aveiro in Portugal, Vinkeveen in the Netherlands, and Nancy, our first event in France. That made 11 events in 2025.

In 2026, Delhi and Mumbai joined Bangalore and Pune, making India the country with the most new cities in recent years. Manila, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore and Curia were back too.

In these years the chain of encounters ran faster than ever. In India, players from Bangalore's first two editions started three more cities within two years.
