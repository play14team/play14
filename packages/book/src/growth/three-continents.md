# Three continents, 2019

For four years, #play14 grew mostly in Europe. In 2019 it reached three new continents within eight months: Sydney in March, Mexico in May and Kuala Lumpur in October.

Each time, it was through someone who had attended an event and wanted to bring it home. Hanna Karlsson played in Madrid and Hamburg, then hosted Sydney. Ulises Aguila played in London, then hosted Mexico. Fred Ducros played in Sydney, then hosted Kuala Lumpur. Nobody pitched #play14 to them.

Europe kept growing too, with Bologna, Lisbon, Vienna and Emmental. Sixteen events were planned for 2019 and **14** took place, the most we had ever had in a single year.

We had no idea how quickly it was about to stop.
