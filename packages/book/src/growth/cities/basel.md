## Where it came from

Bernhard Sterchi played in Milano in 2016 and in London in 2017. In April 2018 he brought #play14 to Switzerland with Andreas Erbe and Peter Stevens, in Basel.

Patrik Kämpf played in Basel that first year and went on to start [Emmental](emmental.md). Bernhard later helped start [Zürich](zurich.md).
