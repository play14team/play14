## Where it came from

In London, I met Karsten Mueller. Julian Kea had played in Luxembourg three years in a row, from the very first edition in 2014. Together they brought #play14 to Hamburg in June 2016, and I came along as their mentor.

Hamburg became one of the great crossroads of the community. In Hamburg I met Mari Luz Garcia, who started [Madrid](madrid.md). Julian and Karsten went on to start [Berlin](berlin.md). Barbara Pauer-Faulmann played in Hamburg in 2018 and started [Vienna](vienna.md). Benjamin Sung Ho Wenzel played there in 2019 and started [Munich](munich.md) with Karsten. And Hanna Karlsson came to Hamburg in 2018, a few weeks after Madrid, on her way to starting [Sydney](sydney.md).
