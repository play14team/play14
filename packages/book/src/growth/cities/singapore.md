## Where it came from

Avi Liran played in Madrid and Kuala Lumpur in 2019. In October 2024 he brought #play14 to Singapore. Darwin Antipolo from Manila and Sripada Nema from Bangalore joined him, along with a local team: Carol Lim, Rajasekhar Tatavarthi, Si Yu Lai, Jodie Loi, Ilamathi Selvarajoo, Yin Mei Ho and Anna Ong. I came as their mentor.

Chompoonuch Tojaroen and Pamas Limpiwatana played in Singapore that first year, and they went home to start [Bangkok](bangkok.md).
