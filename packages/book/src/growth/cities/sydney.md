## Where it came from

In Madrid, I met Hanna Karlsson. She was a Swede living in Australia, back in Europe for the summer of 2018 and looking for interesting conferences. She signed up for Madrid in early June and Hamburg at the end of June.

She was hooked right away and decided to bring #play14 to Sydney. With Catherine Hyams, Hayley Bell and Ralph Warta, she hosted the first event in Oceania in March 2019, and I came as their mentor.

In Sydney, I met Fred Ducros, who started [Kuala Lumpur](kuala-lumpur.md). Hanna came to Kuala Lumpur as his mentor.
