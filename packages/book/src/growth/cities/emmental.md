## Where it came from

Patrik Kämpf played in Basel in 2018, its first year. In October 2019 he started a second Swiss event in Emmental, with Brigitte Woodtli and Elvira Bühlmann. Bernhard Sterchi, who had started Basel, and I came as mentors.
