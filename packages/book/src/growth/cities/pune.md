## Where it came from

Pune came out of the Indian and Malaysian branches together. Kavitha Talreja first played in Kuala Lumpur in 2023, then twice in Bangalore. Imran Shaikh played in Bangalore in 2024, and Chetan Patil in Kuala Lumpur in 2024. In October 2025 they hosted the first Pune edition, mentored by Wan Fadzil Adlan Wan Sidik and Laxman Murugappan.
