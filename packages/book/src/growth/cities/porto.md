## Where it came from

Porto is where three paths met. Ana Umbelino, Ricardo Fernandes and Rita Oliveira Pelica first played in Luxembourg in March 2018. Nuno Mateiro had played in [Barcelona](barcelona.md), and Raquel Pedrouso in [Madrid](madrid.md). Six months later, in September 2018, they were hosting Porto together, and I came as their mentor.

The Portuguese team became one of the most prolific in the community: [Lisbon](lisbon.md), [Viseu](viseu.md), [Curia](curia.md) and [Aveiro](aveiro.md). Ricardo went further still. He mentored new teams in Kuala Lumpur, [Bangalore](bangalore.md) and [Delhi](delhi.md).
