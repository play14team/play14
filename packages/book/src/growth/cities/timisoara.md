## Where it came from

Dragos Marius Jumanca and Leon-Cosmin Lupu first played in Luxembourg in 2015, and they came back in 2016 and 2017. Adriana Rusu and Karina Herman joined them in Luxembourg in 2016. In July 2017 the four of them brought #play14 to Romania, and I came as their mentor.

Five years later, Romania got a second city: [Iasi](iasi.md).
