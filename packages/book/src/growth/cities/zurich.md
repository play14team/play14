## Where it came from

Zürich brought together people from several branches of the family. Bernhard Sterchi had started Basel. Pierre Neis is one of the founders. Nicole Helmerich had just played in Berlin in 2023, and Tetiana Petrukha had played in Madrid in 2018. With Alexandre Cuva and Isatu S Barrie, they hosted the first Zürich edition in September 2023.
