## Where it came from

In Kuala Lumpur, I met Darwin Antipolo. In May 2023 he hosted the first Asian event after the pandemic, in Manila, with Brent Byron Que, Jeng Lamug, Michael Marcel Santos and Laxman Murugappan, who had played in Lisbon and Kuala Lumpur in 2019. I came as their mentor.

The event took place at the Asian Institute of Management Conference Centre. The hosts welcomed everyone with "Mabuhay!" and a page explaining to newcomers what an unconference is.

Darwin and Laxman both became mentors. Darwin went on to mentor [Singapore](singapore.md) and [Bangkok](bangkok.md), and Laxman mentored [Bangalore](bangalore.md), [Pune](pune.md), [Delhi](delhi.md) and [Mumbai](mumbai.md).
