## Where it came from

Mumbai was started by four players from Bangalore. Pankkti Shhah and Imran Shaikh played its first edition in 2024, and Bharti Maru and Vaishali Naik its second, in November 2024. Imran had already helped start Pune, and Bharti Delhi. In March 2026 they hosted the first Mumbai edition, mentored by Wan Fadzil Adlan Wan Sidik and Laxman Murugappan.
