## Where it came from

Barbara Pauer-Faulmann played in Hamburg in 2018. A year later, in September 2019, she brought #play14 to Austria with Anton Jessner, Barbara Kühnelt, Michael Laussegger and Mike Leber, and Pierre Neis came as their mentor.
