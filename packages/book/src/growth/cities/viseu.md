## Where it came from

In June 2022, as events slowly came back, the Porto team of Ana Umbelino, Nuno Mateiro and Ricardo Fernandes started a third Portuguese city, Viseu.

Pedro Figueiredo played there and joined the team that started [Curia](curia.md) the following year.
