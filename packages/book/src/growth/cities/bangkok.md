## Where it came from

Chompoonuch Tojaroen (Chompoo) and Pamas Limpiwatana (Pam) played in Singapore in 2024. A year later, in November 2025, they hosted the first Bangkok edition with Panupan Peasura (Jo), Tanawat Akharametawee (Top), Poopha Teerachotpokin, Tanatporn Kunarayakul (Mai), Bencharatana Jongjumruspun (Neat) and Ranida Nuangjhamnong. Avi Liran from Singapore, Darwin Antipolo from Manila and I came as mentors.

A first Bangkok edition had been planned for 2020 and was canceled because of the pandemic. It took five more years, but it happened.
