## Where it came from

A year after Hamburg, Julian Kea and Karsten Mueller started a second German city, Berlin, in June 2017, this time with Cori Moore.

Berlin kept that chain going. Nicole Helmerich played there in 2023 and joined the team that started [Zürich](zurich.md).
