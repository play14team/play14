## Where it came from

In June 2019, less than a year after their first edition, the Porto team started a second Portuguese city, Lisbon.

One of the players in Lisbon that year was Laxman Murugappan. He played again in Kuala Lumpur a few months later, and he went on to help start [Manila](manila.md) and to mentor most of the Indian cities.
