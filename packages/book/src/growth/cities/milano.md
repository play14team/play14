## Where it came from

Giovanni Puliti played at the very first #play14 in Luxembourg in 2014, and came back in 2015 and 2016. In May 2016 he brought it home to Milano with Fabio Ghislandi and Giulio Roggero.

The Milano team turned out to be one of the most prolific in the community. They went on to start [Bari](bari.md), [Bologna](bologna.md), [Figino Serenza](figino-serenza.md) and [Florence](florence.md). Milano also welcomed players who started cities of their own: Bernhard Sterchi, who started [Basel](basel.md), and Esther Balmaña and Francesc Mas, who started [Barcelona](barcelona.md).
