## Where it came from

In November 2023, four players from India travelled to Kuala Lumpur: Sripada Nema, Sumit Sethi, Senthil Kumar G and Atul Jain. Five months later, in April 2024, they hosted the first #play14 in India, in Bangalore, with Laxman Murugappan and Wan Fadzil Adlan Wan Sidik. Ricardo Fernandes, who had started Porto, came as their mentor.

Bangalore was the start of a wave. Players from its first two editions went on to start [Pune](pune.md), [Delhi](delhi.md) and [Mumbai](mumbai.md).
