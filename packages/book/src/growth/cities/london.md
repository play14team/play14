## Where it came from

In March 2015, at the second Luxembourg edition, I met Chris Caswell and Christina Ohanian, who had come over from London.

At first we never imagined #play14 would spread to other cities. Our plan was to make the Luxembourg event bigger every year. But Chris and Christina wanted the same thing at home, and it became clear that with their motivation and our help they could take the format to the UK: the same experience for participants, with a slightly different British flavor.

It worked. "The two Chrises", with Chris Selby and Mark Webb, recreated what they had experienced in Luxembourg, and the players in London enjoyed it just as much. That is when #play14 started to grow wider instead of bigger.

London went on to welcome players who started their own cities: Karsten Mueller in 2015, who started [Hamburg](hamburg.md), and Ulises Aguila in 2018, who started [Mexico](mexico.md).
