## Where it came from

Pierre Hervouet played at the very first #play14, in Luxembourg in 2014, and he was a friend of the other Pierre, Pierre Neis. When he wanted to try running a #play14 in Beirut, we supported him, and Pierre Neis hosted it with him.

The Beirut edition lasted only one day, but people loved it all the same. Two months after London, #play14 had left Europe for the first time. That is when we began to see that learning through play wasn't tied to one culture. The games, the debriefs and the unconference format worked in Lebanon just as they had in Luxembourg and London.
