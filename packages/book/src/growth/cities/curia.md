## Where it came from

In September 2023, the Portuguese team of Ana Umbelino, Nuno Mateiro and Ricardo Fernandes started Curia, with Pedro Figueiredo, who had played his first #play14 in Viseu the year before. Curia came back in 2024 and 2026, and has already announced its 2027 date.
