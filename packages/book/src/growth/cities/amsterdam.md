## Where it came from

Koen van der Pasch and Sonja Uittenboogaard first played in Luxembourg in March 2017. By November they had brought it to Amsterdam, with Anko Tijman, Jordann Gross, Ralph van Roosmalen and Stanislava Potupchik, one of the largest founding teams we had seen. I came as their mentor.

The Dutch team kept going for years. It started [Utrecht](utrecht.md), the first event after the pandemic, then [Zoetermeer](zoetermeer.md) and [Vinkeveen](vinkeveen.md). Eddy Bruin and Theo Geelen, who played their first #play14 in Amsterdam in 2017, became part of it.
