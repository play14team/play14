## Where it came from

Ulises Aguila played at #play14 London in 2018. The following year he decided to bring it to Mexico, the first event in the Americas. He put together a strong team with Adriana Molina, Alex Canizales Castro and Mario Méndez López, and Pierre Neis came as their mentor.
