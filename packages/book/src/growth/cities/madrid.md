## Where it came from

In Hamburg, I met Mari Luz Garcia. It was her first #play14. A year later, in June 2017, she brought it to Madrid with Beatriz Gortazar, Meryl His and Sophie Vurpillot, and I came as their mentor.

Madrid became a hub. Hanna Karlsson played there in 2018 and started [Sydney](sydney.md). Manolo Lopez played in 2017 and started [Barcelona](barcelona.md). Raquel Pedrouso played in 2017 and joined the team that started [Porto](porto.md). Catalina Vrabie played in 2019 and started [Iasi](iasi.md). Avi Liran played in 2019 and started [Singapore](singapore.md). Tetiana Petrukha played in 2018 and joined the team that started [Zürich](zurich.md).
