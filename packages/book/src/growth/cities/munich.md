## Where it came from

Benjamin Sung Ho Wenzel played in Hamburg in 2019. In October 2022 he started Munich with Karsten Mueller, who had started Hamburg six years earlier. A first Munich edition had been planned for 2020 and was canceled because of the pandemic.
