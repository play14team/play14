## Where it came from

Barcelona came from two directions at once. Esther Balmaña and Francesc Mas played in Milano in May 2017, and Manolo Lopez played in Madrid a month later (Esther was there too). By October 2017 they had brought #play14 to Barcelona, with Mònica Valcárcel and Yuval Sidney Barak Azagury.

Barcelona then passed it on. Nuno Mateiro played his first #play14 there, and a year later he was part of the team that started [Porto](porto.md).
