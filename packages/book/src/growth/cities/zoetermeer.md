## Where it came from

In September 2024, the Dutch team of Anko Tijman, Eddy Bruin, Jordann Gross, Koen van der Pasch and Theo Geelen started a third Dutch city, Zoetermeer.

Diana Vredenbregt played there and joined the team that started [Vinkeveen](vinkeveen.md).
