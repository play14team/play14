## Where it came from

In Sydney, I met Fred Ducros. He was French and lived and worked in Kuala Lumpur, and he decided it was time for #play14 in Asia. In October 2019 he hosted the first event in Asia with Andrew Lau, Narudee (Poom) Kristhanin, Shadaitul Intan and Sufiz Mohd Suffian. Hanna Karlsson, who had just started Sydney, and I came as mentors.

Kuala Lumpur became the seed for most of Asia. In Kuala Lumpur, I met Darwin Antipolo, who started [Manila](manila.md). Laxman Murugappan, Wan Fadzil Adlan Wan Sidik and Avi Liran played there in 2019 too. Four years later, in 2023, four players from India (Sripada Nema, Sumit Sethi, Senthil Kumar G and Atul Jain) came to Kuala Lumpur and went home to start [Bangalore](bangalore.md).
