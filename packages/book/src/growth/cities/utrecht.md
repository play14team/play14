## Where it came from

The Dutch team from Amsterdam took on the responsibility of running the first event after the pandemic. A short window opened at the end of 2021, and Anko Tijman, Eddy Bruin, Koen van der Pasch, Sonja Uittenboogaard and Theo Geelen used it for a safe, joyful and of course playful event in Utrecht.

It felt so good to be back, and it left us wanting more.
