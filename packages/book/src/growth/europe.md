# Across Europe, 2017–2018

By 2017 the format had proven it could travel. Eight new cities joined in two years. More and more often, the new organizers hadn't played in Luxembourg at all: they had discovered #play14 in Hamburg, Milano, Madrid or Barcelona.

That's when the family tree started to branch. Hamburg led to Madrid and Berlin, Milano to Barcelona and Basel, and Madrid and Barcelona both fed into Porto. Eight events took place in 2017 and eleven in 2018.

So many new teams needed help that mentoring became a habit, and soon a rule. That story is told in [the mentoring program](../mentoring/why-mentoring.md).
