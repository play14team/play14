# Twelve years on

## Ten years

In March 2024 we went back to where it all started: the Technoport in Esch/Belval, for our 10th anniversary. The invitation spoke of close to 2,500 players who had joined us over ten years.

We did a few things we had never done before. There was a big anniversary party on Friday night, with cake and many, many games. We started building something together as a community, meant to be passed on to the next event. And for the first time, kids officially joined: on Saturday, young players between 7 and 17 came to discover #play14, with a quiet room to retreat to and permission to propose their own games.

## Today

As I write in early October 2026, the numbers are these: **93 events** have taken place in **40 cities** across **20 countries**, in Europe, Asia, Oceania and the Americas. Madrid is under way this very weekend, and Bangkok and London are coming in November. Curia has already announced its 2027 date.
## In 14 words

On March 14th, 2026, exactly twelve years after the first event in Luxembourg, I recorded a talk for GamiCon 38 called *#play14 in 14 words*. I wanted to explain what #play14 is to people who had never been to one.

Those fourteen words make up [the last part of this book](../special/index.md). Before that, there's one more part of the story: how experienced hosts help new ones get started.
