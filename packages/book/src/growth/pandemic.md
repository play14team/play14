# The pandemic years

## The first cancellation

<img class="story-image" src="https://cdn.play14.org/strapi-uploads/assets/luxembourg_a849940fb4.jpg" alt="Luxembourg">

*February 2020*

With a heavy heart, we canceled the 2020 Luxembourg event because of COVID-19. It turned out to be the right decision: the authorities imposed a lockdown on March 15th. It was the first of many cancellations.

An event built on being in the same room, playing with your hands and eating at the same table seemed impossible to run remotely.

## The first online event

<img class="story-image" src="https://cdn.play14.org/strapi-uploads/assets/online_5c071c3142.jpg" alt="An online session">

*May 9th, 2020*

After the initial panic, which everyone went through, we started looking for ways to recreate online what we did in real life. We wanted some normality back, and we didn't want to lose our momentum.

The team that was supposed to host Stuttgart, with Pierre's help, organized our first online event. Using Miro and Zoom, they brought together #play14 alumni and newcomers. We were all figuring out how to translate our games for a screen, and we learned a lot that day. I captured some of it in an article: [the first online #play14 ever](https://play14.org/articles/first-online-play14-ever).

## The big one

<img class="story-image" src="https://cdn.play14.org/strapi-uploads/assets/bigone_61a3c1d74c.jpg" alt="The big one">

*July 25th, 2020*

By then we had hosting teams all over the world, and none of them could travel. Pierre suggested using that: a 24-hour online event that would follow the sun around the planet.

It started in Sydney at 9 AM local time and ended at 4 PM in Mexico and Los Angeles. Hosts in each time zone took turns facilitating.

Eighteen hosts shared the facilitation, more than any #play14 before or since.

It could only have happened because of the years before. By the summer of 2020, enough hosts existed on enough continents to keep a 24-hour event going.

## The cost

The numbers tell how hard those years were. In 2019, 14 events took place. In 2020, 15 events were canceled, and only three took place. In 2021 only one did. Teams in Prague, Bangkok, Munich, Leeds, Stuttgart and Utrecht had everything ready and had to wait, some for years. A few of them never got their first edition.
