# The first circle, 2015–2016

For its first year, #play14 was a Luxembourg event. Then people started coming to Luxembourg from elsewhere, and leaving with the idea of doing it at home.

The first four cities all trace back directly to Luxembourg, or to the people we met there. In 2015, Chris Caswell and Christina Ohanian took it to London, and Pierre Hervouet to Beirut. In 2016, Giovanni Puliti took it to Milano, and Julian Kea and Karsten Mueller to Hamburg.
