# A meeting in a hotel bar

<img class="story-image" src="https://cdn.play14.org/strapi-uploads/assets/alpha_mercure_1cefe1ae05.jpg" alt="The Alpha Mercure hotel in Luxembourg">

*December 2013*

The story starts with an event we couldn't attend.

[play4agile](https://play4agile.wordpress.com/) was a German unconference about games and agile, and in 2013 it was the place to be if you cared about learning through play. Tickets for the next edition sold out within a few hours, and three of us in Luxembourg didn't get one: Yann Gensollen, Pierre Neis and me, Cédric Pontet.

We met at the bar of the Alpha Mercure hotel to talk it over, and the conversation soon turned to a different question: why not organize a playful event of our own?

It turned out we didn't need much: a venue, a date and a good name. We picked the date that night: **March 14th, 2014**.

The name took longer, and we came up with it together. Our first idea was just the number, but we couldn't register a domain name like "14.org". So we put "play" in front of it, which said what the event was about anyway. Then we added the hashtag, so nobody would ever have to ask "what's the hashtag?". And yes, the hashtag is part of the name.

People still ask us why 14. The official answer is: why not?
