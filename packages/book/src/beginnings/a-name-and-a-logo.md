# A name and a logo

<img class="story-image" src="https://cdn.play14.org/strapi-uploads/assets/play14_500x500_black_bg_4d6f0b089f.png" alt="The #play14 logo on a black background">

*January 2014*

Having a name was a good start, but we needed a logo before we could promote the event. I asked a co-worker, [Christian Jolas](https://www.linkedin.com/in/christianjolas), and he generously gave his own time to sketch several proposals.

After some hesitation we went for the simplest and most colorful one, and we have never regretted it. We first saw it printed on black t-shirts, and we knew right away it was the right choice. Looking at the photos after the first event, we realized Christian had given us more than a logo: he had given us a visual identity.

The five colors (red, orange, yellow, green and blue) have been with us ever since. They are on the website, the t-shirts and the badges, and you'll find them in the theme of this book too.
