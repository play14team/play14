# Once was not enough

<img class="story-image" src="https://cdn.play14.org/strapi-uploads/assets/luxembourg_a849940fb4.jpg" alt="Luxembourg">

*March 27th, 2015*

We were so happy with the first event that we announced the second one before the first had ended. We asked Diego whether we could book a date for the following year, and he said yes.

A lot of players from 2014 came back for the second edition, which told us we were onto something. People weren't there for a one-off novelty. They wanted to come back and play again with the same group.

That edition also brought two new faces from London: Chris Caswell and Christina Ohanian. We didn't know it then, but meeting them changed the direction of #play14.
