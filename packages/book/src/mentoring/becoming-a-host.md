# Becoming a host

There are only two rules for hosting a #play14 event.

## The first rule: play first

**You must have attended at least one #play14 event.**

That gives you a feel for the community and the mindset we try to create. We believe you can only reproduce #play14 well if you've experienced it yourself.

Every story in this book follows that rule. Chris and Christina played in Luxembourg before London. Pierre Hervouet played in Luxembourg before Beirut. Hanna played in Madrid and Hamburg before Sydney. Ulises played in London before Mexico. Fred played in Sydney before Kuala Lumpur.

## The second rule: find a mentor

**You need a mentor to guide you the first time.**

As a host, you're responsible for keeping the spirit of the original event and giving your participants an experience they remember. A mentor helps you do that.

## Finding a mentor

Mentors are community members who have hosted #play14 events and can help others do the same. To find one, contact the mentors group and someone will reply. You can also browse the [players list](https://play14.org/players?position=mentor) and filter by the mentor role.

## What a host is responsible for

The [hosting guide](https://play14.org/events/hosting) covers it in detail. In short, the host:

- secures a venue
- picks a date
- provides food and drinks
- gathers game materials
- brings a positive attitude

The rest is up to the players. We recommend a team of at least three local organizers, which makes hosting much easier to manage, especially when choosing a venue.
