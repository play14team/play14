# Why mentoring

#play14 has no head office that approves new cities, no franchise fee and no marketing team. Still, a #play14 in Kuala Lumpur feels like one in Luxembourg. The mentoring program explains why.

## What London taught us

When Chris and Christina brought #play14 to London in 2015, they didn't start from a manual. They had played at the event, and we helped them recreate it. That turned out to be the right way to pass the format on.

A lot of what makes #play14 work never shows up in a checklist: the first evening, the marketplace energy in the morning, a good debrief, the moment the hosts stop organizing and start playing. You learn those by experiencing them and by having someone beside you the first time you run them.

So every new host gets a mentor: someone who has already hosted a #play14 event and helps them do it for the first time.

## Wider, not bigger

Mentoring is how we grow *organically*. We never push anyone to host an event. People come to us, usually after playing at one, because they want the same thing at home. The community then supports them.

That keeps growth slow, and on purpose. A new event only appears when someone local cares enough to host it, and a mentor is available to help. That has kept the spirit intact across a dozen years and 20 countries.

## From habit to rule

In the early years, mentoring wasn't a program. It was simply what we did. One of the co-founders went to London in 2015, to Hamburg in 2016, and to Madrid, Timisoara and Amsterdam in 2017, to help the first edition along.

By 2018 there were too many new teams for three or four founders to support alone. Around 2018 and 2019, mentoring became an explicit rule for new hosts, and experienced hosts started mentoring too. In 2019, Bernhard Sterchi, who had hosted Basel, mentored the first Emmental edition. Hanna Karlsson, who had just hosted Sydney, mentored Kuala Lumpur. Pierre mentored Mexico and Vienna. That year 14 events took place, the most we had ever had.

## Mentors today

Today the website lists 32 mentors alongside the four founders. Almost all of them first hosted an event in their own city: Milano, Hamburg, Amsterdam, Porto, Berlin, Madrid, Kuala Lumpur, Manila, Bangalore. You can find them on the [players list](https://play14.org/players?position=mentor).
