# What a mentor does

A mentor doesn't run your event for you. The mentor is the experienced player beside you who has seen the format work many times and can tell when something is off.

## Before the event

The global #play14 organization shares its resources with new hosts, and co-founders and mentors help along the way. Nobody hosts alone.

Local organizers talk to each other on a Slack channel and in a WhatsApp community, and we hold video calls when a team needs more support. That's where a new host asks how much food to order, how to price tickets, or what to do if the venue cancels.

## During the event

**At least one co-founder or mentor attends each new event.** They help with the organization and make sure the essence of #play14 is respected.

Because of that, the hosting guide asks new teams to set aside part of their budget for the mentor's travel and accommodation. Choosing a mentor who lives nearby keeps those costs down and makes communication easier.

## After the event

Mentoring doesn't stop when the event ends. The host team runs its own retrospective, publishes the results and announces the next date. Hosts who have done it a few times can then mentor the next new team.

That cycle is how #play14 spreads: a player becomes a host, and a host becomes a mentor.

## A chain across Asia

The best illustration of the cycle is what happened in Asia and India after the pandemic.

- **Kuala Lumpur, 2019.** Fred Ducros brings #play14 to Asia after playing in Sydney. Among the hosts is Sufiz Mohd Suffian.
- **Manila, 2023.** The first Asian event after the pandemic. Laxman Murugappan and Darwin Antipolo are on the hosting team.
- **Kuala Lumpur, 2023.** Wan Fadzil Adlan Wan Sidik hosts, with Ricardo Fernandes as mentor. Ricardo first hosted Porto in 2018. Five years later he was mentoring on another continent.
- **Bangalore, 2024.** India's first #play14, mentored by Ricardo. Sripada Nema is one of the hosts.
- **Bangalore, November 2024.** Its mentors are Ricardo, Laxman and Wan Fadzil: two hosts from Manila and Kuala Lumpur, now mentoring.
- **Singapore 2024, Bangkok 2025.** Avi Liran hosts Singapore, then mentors Bangkok with Darwin from Manila.
- **Pune 2025, Delhi 2026, Mumbai 2026.** Laxman mentors all three new Indian cities, with Wan Fadzil at Pune and Mumbai and Ricardo at Delhi.
- **Kuala Lumpur 2025, Singapore 2025, Manila 2026.** Sufiz, a host since 2019, now mentors in three countries.

None of this was planned from Luxembourg. Players became hosts, hosts became mentors, and each mentor brought the next city along.
