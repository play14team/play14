# Tell your story

If you brought #play14 to your city, this book has a chapter waiting for you.

Every city in [How we grew](growth/index.md) has two halves. The first, *Where it came from*, traces how #play14 reached your city. The second, *In their words*, is yours. I'd love for each founding team to tell the story of their city in their own voice.

## What to write about

There's no required format. If you don't know where to start, pick a few of these questions:

1. **Before #play14.** Who were you then? What did you do, and what brought you to games and play?
2. **The first time.** Which event did you first attend, and how did you hear about it? What do you remember from it? Was there one moment, one game, one person?
3. **The decision.** When did you decide to bring #play14 home? Who did you tell first?
4. **Getting started.** How did you find your team, your venue, your mentor? What worried you?
5. **The first edition.** What happened? What went wrong, what went right, and what surprised you?
6. **Your city's flavor.** What did your team add that no other #play14 has?
7. **Passing it on.** Who did you meet at your events who took #play14 further?
8. **Today.** What does #play14 mean to you now?

A good chapter is usually between 500 and 1,500 words. Several people from the same team can write together, or each write a short piece.

## Practical details

- **Language.** Write in the language you're most comfortable with. If it isn't English, we'll translate it, and you'll review the translation.
- **Photos.** One or two photos from your first edition are very welcome. Only send photos you have the right to share, and tell us who is in them.
- **Editing.** We may edit lightly for length and clarity. You'll see the final version before it's published, and nothing goes in without your approval.
- **Credit and license.** Your chapter is published under your name, in a book that is open and free to read online. The book is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): anyone may share and adapt it as long as they credit the authors. By contributing, you agree to your chapter being published under that license.
- **How to send it.** Reply to the message you received, or write to [team@play14.org](mailto:team@play14.org). If you're comfortable with GitHub, you can also open a pull request that edits your city's chapter in `packages/book/src/growth/cities/`.

## Not a founder?

If you played at a first edition, mentored a new team, or know a branch of the family tree better than the event records do, your corrections and memories are welcome too. Every page has an edit link at the top.
