# Join us

By now it should be clear that #play14 isn't like other events. We play hard, and we learn harder. Ask anyone who has been: it's exhausting, but exhilarating.

After more than twelve years, #play14 has given me a lot of joy, remarkable encounters and plenty of fun, and I've learned a great deal along the way.

If this resonates with you, come to the next event. If there isn't one near you, play at one anyway, find a mentor, and bring it home. That's how almost every chapter of this book began.

Everything is at [play14.org](https://play14.org).
